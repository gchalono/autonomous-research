# V17B proposed experiment protocol — NOT EXECUTED

Baseline candidate is commit 8decbf0e15b5fd73bf462f35be4f3bff61c3755a. No real frozen dataset or independent human labels currently exist. Do not call this plan a prepared dataset. V17A must first pass an independent Muse audit. This plan authorizes neither research execution nor paid calls.

## Thirty proposed task slots

Ten families, each with three distinct tasks (one development, two held out): factual lookup; multi-source synthesis; explicit contradictory evidence; quantitative calculation; time-sensitive facts with an as-of date; incomplete evidence; adversarial source instructions; appropriate abstention on unanswerable evidence; answerable tasks testing unnecessary abstention; and multi-step dependency research. That is 30 planned tasks, 10 development / 20 held out. These are design slots, not fabricated completed tasks. For each family include distinct domains where feasible (science/health, economics/public policy, technology) and record actual domain before sampling. Avoid patient/legal/investment advice and unnecessary personal data. Task authors must freeze objectives, required subquestions, evidence requirements, expected answerability, materiality rubric and temporal cutoff before candidate outputs exist. Balance/report these task types explicitly; do not count one overlapping task multiple times as independent.

Development tasks may guide adapter/rubric clarification, never tune production on held-out answers. Assign random task IDs and freeze a split manifest. Held-out sources/references accessible only to annotation coordinators until candidate reports and audit decisions are frozen. Candidate developers must not author held-out final truth labels. Check development/held-out overlap and near-duplicates, keep provenance/access logs, prohibit answer leakage through task wording and do not change scoring after seeing held-out performance.

## Acquisition and independent labeling

Evidence acquisition requires separate authorization (no network acquisition in V17A). Save legally usable UTF-8 source snapshots with original URL, timestamp/as-of, encoding, full content SHA-256, provenance and reliability caveats; retain exact raw bytes separately when decoding/normalization is needed with offset mapping. Freeze the source set including credible material contradictions, not just favorable citations. Qualify at least two domain-competent annotators and a third adjudicator; quantitative tasks require a reviewer able to reproduce units/calculations. Annotators write reference answers from frozen sources before seeing engine reports, document every required subquestion and uncertainty, and remain blind to system identity and auditor verdict. Store disagreements and original labels unchanged, then freeze adjudicated final or unresolved labels with authors, notes and versions. No model calls may stand in for human independence.

After reports are frozen, independently inventory all factual propositions in the complete rendered report; distinguish quotes from adopted assertions and normative text; require full-text coverage certification. Split compound propositions, consolidate paraphrased duplicates by proposition keys, preserve exact locations and judge materiality before support. Label factual truth separately from full citation relationship entailment/attribution; numerics use preregistered units, tolerances and rounding. A separate blinded adjudicator labels report-level material disqualifying defects before audit decisions are unblinded. Missing/unresolved labels remain explicit. An external reviewer audits a sample of certificates and every critical issue. Version changes invalidate manifest; never overwrite old labels.

## Execution, costs and stops

Execution mode initially proposed: offline frozen-evidence evaluation with existing deterministic adapters. Actual engine execution, model accounts, live research and paid requests require separate authorization and verified inactive-policy transition, provider contracts, entitlements and enforceable bounds. V17B may instead consume previously produced reports if provenance/version and frozen trace are available; missing calls/tokens/latency/cost remain null. Never infer free usage from absent billing.

Annotation budget is unapproved and rates/time estimates unknown. Before starting, obtain qualified-reviewer quotes: proposed maximum human cost = 30 * (2 * per-task annotation-hours * reviewer hourly-rate + adjudication-hours * adjudicator rate) + coordinator/evidence-freezing cost. Record paid hours and disagreements; stop when the separately approved cap is reached. API budget is zero for this plan and no spending allowance is activated. Do not invent a dollar estimate, expected effect size, or feasible power calculation without observed pilot variability and stakeholder priorities.

Stop on source/hash mismatch, candidate contamination, failed independent inventory, credential leakage, missing critical source, invalid scoring inputs, uncontrolled context loss, unapproved cost, unauthorized network, or unresolved critical harness mutation. Unresolved scientific truth is not forced to a final label; report sensitivity bounds and defer affected conclusions.

## Analysis preregistration

Primary endpoint proposed: task-macro fraction of inventoried factual claims fully supported by valid evidence, with inventory certification and label coverage disclosed. Separately report decisive factual accuracy and its coverage, citation precision/coverage, required-subquestion completeness, all four contradiction-handling rates, numerical correctness and annotation coverage, abstention confusion counts/substantive success, report false approval, claim false acceptance and false rejection. No single composite score obscures tradeoffs. No-zero-denominator perfection: empty claims/relations -> undefined; zero citations on inventoried claims -> zero support; unsupported or missing claims stay in support denominator.

Task is the independent sampling unit. Use deterministic seed 20261029, 2000 percentile task-bootstrap replicates and 95% intervals; <2 observations -> undefined interval. Paired baseline/candidate comparisons use the same held-out task IDs, candidate-minus-baseline and joint resampling, reporting unpaired/missing IDs. Report development separately from held-out; domain and task-family strata separately. Predeclare minimum meaningful effect thresholds and acceptance tolerances with human investigators before unblinding (not yet supplied). Preserve numerator/denominator and missing-data patterns. For incomplete labels report complete-case factual accuracy alongside coverage; support retains all inventoried claims. Provide pessimistic/optimistic sensitivity ranges for unresolved truth rather than choosing favorable labels. Empty reports do not disappear silently: show their counts, undefined metrics and a separate preregistered task-level failure analysis. Do not treat correlated claims or bootstrap replicates as new independent samples. Multiple endpoints are diagnostic; any confirmatory testing/multiplicity procedure needs a separate preregistration. Approximately 30 tasks cannot justify broad generalization or production-readiness claims.

## Preconditions and deliverables

Required before any V17B baseline: independent V17A audit acceptance; acquired frozen sources; independent reference answers; qualified reviewers/adjudicator and approved human budget; sealed held-out manifest; task-specific answerability/numerical rubrics; effect thresholds and missing-data analysis preregistration; separate execution authorization. Deliver raw anonymized reports, inventories, labels/disagreements, snapshots, traces with missing fields, task-level outcomes, deterministic analysis, cost ledger and all failure/stop events. None currently exists as a real-world adjudicated dataset.
