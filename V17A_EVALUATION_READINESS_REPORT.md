## Measurement contract and architecture

Distinct stable-ID entities: task, subquestion, frozen source, evidence passage, reference answer, engine report, factual claim, citation, claim annotation, citation relationship annotation, auditor/issue, numerical reference/judgment, contradiction/judgment, abstention, operational trace and evaluation result. Dataset version/schema/scorer/candidate version, sealed full-input manifest, configuration, optional explicit timestamp and environment are retained. Altering any frozen field invalidates the digest; study authors must mint versions and preserve old labels. Offsets are exact Unicode code-point half-open spans with no normalization. The entire passage must match, so genuine-prefix/fabricated-continuation fails validation. Unknown labels, malformed graphs, duplicate IDs, unsupported versions and nonfinite values are invalid, not plausible quality scores.

Claim universe: inventoried, submitted, annotated, evidence-resolvable and finalized subsets stay distinct. Lossless field-path rendering, full-text partition and exact locations detects internal omissions/gaps and mismatch with engine payload. Atomicity/semantic deduplication are a human annotation protocol supported by proposition keys and exact normalized duplicate rejection; this cannot detect a dishonest completeness attestation or all paraphrases automatically. Independent inventory is a V17B prerequisite. All shipped certificates/labels are synthetic by construction, never independently authenticated human adjudication. Final unverified-origin labels are rejected; declared origin is still not identity proof. No comprehensive report-accuracy claim follows from a green suite or a structurally complete certificate.

Metrics: decisive factual correctness and adjudication coverage; full claim support; deduplicated relationship citation precision plus relationship-label coverage; adequate citation coverage and citation presence; conditional report false approval, explicit claim false acceptance and false rejection; required-question coverage; four contradiction-handling rates; abstention confusion categories and substantive success; numerical correctness/coverage using exact finite decimals, absolute/relative bounds, literal units and HALF_EVEN rounding; nullable operational measurements. Empty denominators are undefined/null, never perfect. No composite reliability score rewards always-abstain. Gold clean report metadata must agree with full finalized evidence, subquestions, numerics, contradictions and unresolved issues.

The three result states are valid, insufficient_adjudication and invalid_input. Missing operational values are null and enumerated, not invented zeros.
